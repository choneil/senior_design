		x-axis interface +x as it meets component gantry at its respective resting place^*
	
	*^axes of movement separate definition for boundaries: y axis is large scale movement
	  bounded by:
		y-axis limits for each respective gantry^*

	*^axes of movement boundaries (xy movement) solder gantry: 
	  xy movement for solder gantry is between:
		 solder toolhead resting place
		 Z-axis normal to the pad at which solder is being placed^*

	*^axes of movement boundaries (xy movement component gantry:
	  xy movement for component gantry is between:
		component toolhead resting place
		center of current component to be picked as it is fed by component feeder
		Z-axis normal to center of component on board as defined by 2 axis weighted average (?) of pins for component^*
